About Us

Durban's favourite decorators since 2018

Our History

Founded in 2018 after our daughter's graduation party in Durban. What started with balloons and fairy lights in our garage has grown into a full decor team serving KZN.

Our Mission

To bring joy and beauty to every celebration with affordable, bright and memorable decor that makes every moment shine.

Our Vision

To be the most loved event decor brand in South Africa — known for creativity, reliability and colour.
