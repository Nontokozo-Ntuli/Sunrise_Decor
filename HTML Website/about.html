About Sunrise Decor

Our History

Sunrise Decor was founded in 2018 by the Bhele family after decorating their daughter's graduation party in Durban. What started in a garage with balloons and fairy lights has grown into a full-service event decor company.

Our Mission

To be Durban's most trusted event styling company, creating moments that shine for every celebration.
